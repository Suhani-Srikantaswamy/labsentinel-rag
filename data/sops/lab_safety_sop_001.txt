LABORATORY SAFETY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: SOP-LAB-001
Title: General Laboratory Safety Rules and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements
Revision: 4.2 | Effective Date: 2024-01-15

=============================================================================
SECTION 1: PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
=============================================================================

1.1 MANDATORY PPE IN ALL LABORATORY AREAS
All personnel entering the laboratory MUST wear the following at all times:
  - Safety goggles (ANSI Z87.1 rated) or chemical splash goggles when handling liquids
  - Lab coat (cotton or flame-resistant material) — fully buttoned, covering arms to wrist
  - Closed-toe shoes with no exposed skin on feet
  - Gloves appropriate to the hazard class of chemicals being used

Violation of PPE requirements is a Level 3 Safety Infraction subject to immediate removal
from the laboratory and disciplinary action.

1.2 GLOVE SELECTION GUIDE
  - Nitrile (blue/purple): Suitable for most common lab chemicals, acids, bases
  - Neoprene (green): Chlorinated solvents, phenol, strong acids
  - Latex: General purpose only — check for allergies before use
  - Thick rubber: Cryogenic materials, concentrated acids

1.3 EYE PROTECTION
Safety glasses provide impact protection only. Chemical splash goggles MUST be worn when:
  - Opening, pouring, or transferring any liquid chemical
  - Working with pressurised systems
  - Performing centrifugation
  - Handling glassware under vacuum or pressure

=============================================================================
SECTION 2: CHEMICAL HANDLING AND STORAGE
=============================================================================

2.1 GENERAL CHEMICAL HANDLING RULES
  a. Never pipette by mouth.
  b. Label all containers immediately upon preparation.
  c. Use secondary containment for all liquids exceeding 1 litre.
  d. Do not use damaged glassware — inspect before each use.
  e. Never work alone with highly toxic or explosive materials.

2.2 FLAMMABLE AND COMBUSTIBLE MATERIALS
  - Store flammable liquids ONLY in approved flammable storage cabinets.
  - Quantities outside the cabinet must not exceed 10 litres per bench area.
  - No open flames within 3 metres of flammable liquid storage or use.
  - Use spark-free equipment and explosion-proof refrigerators for flammable storage.

2.3 ACID AND BASE HANDLING
  - Always add acid TO water, never water to acid (exothermic reaction risk).
  - Store acids and bases in separate, dedicated cabinets.
  - Perform dilutions in a fume hood when working with concentrated acids.
  - Have neutralising agent (sodium bicarbonate for acids, boric acid for bases) readily accessible.

=============================================================================
SECTION 3: FUME HOOD OPERATION
=============================================================================

3.1 REQUIRED FUME HOOD USE
The following operations MUST be performed inside a functioning fume hood:
  - Use of volatile organic solvents (acetone, ethanol > 10%, methanol, ether)
  - Preparation or use of concentrated acids (HCl, H2SO4, HNO3, HF)
  - Any procedure generating aerosols, dusts, or toxic vapours
  - Heating reactions that may produce vapours

3.2 FUME HOOD OPERATING PROCEDURE
  a. Verify the fume hood airflow indicator is active (green light or bead chain moving) BEFORE starting work.
  b. Position the sash at or below the marked working height line (typically 45 cm).
  c. Keep all materials at least 15 cm behind the sash plane.
  d. Do not store materials permanently in the fume hood — this obstructs airflow.
  e. Report non-functioning fume hoods to the Safety Officer IMMEDIATELY — do not use.

3.3 FUME HOOD FAILURE PROTOCOL
If a fume hood alarm sounds or airflow fails during use:
  1. Stop work immediately
  2. Close all open containers
  3. Leave the sash in the lowered position
  4. Evacuate the immediate area if vapours are present
  5. Call the Safety Officer: Ext. 4-SAFE (4-7233)

=============================================================================
SECTION 4: EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
=============================================================================

4.1 CHEMICAL SPILL RESPONSE
  Minor spill (< 1 litre, non-toxic, non-volatile):
    1. Alert others in the area
    2. Don appropriate PPE
    3. Use spill kit to absorb and neutralise
    4. Dispose of waste in appropriate container
    5. Complete an incident report within 24 hours

  Major spill or toxic/flammable material:
    1. Evacuate the laboratory immediately
    2. Pull fire alarm if fire risk is present
    3. Call Emergency Services: 911
    4. Call Campus Safety: Ext. 4-SAFE
    5. Do NOT re-enter until cleared by hazmat team

4.2 FIRE RESPONSE
  - Know the location of the nearest fire extinguisher before starting work.
  - For small, contained fires (trash can, small bench fire): Use PASS technique with CO2 extinguisher.
  - For large fires or fires involving chemicals: Activate alarm, evacuate immediately, call 911.
  - Never use water on chemical or electrical fires.

4.3 INJURY RESPONSE
  - Eye exposure: Irrigate immediately at eyewash station for minimum 15 minutes.
  - Skin exposure: Wash with soap and water for minimum 15 minutes; remove contaminated clothing.
  - Inhalation: Move to fresh air; call 911 if symptoms persist.
  - Cuts or punctures: Apply pressure, seek medical attention immediately for any chemically-contaminated wound.

=============================================================================
SECTION 5: WASTE DISPOSAL
=============================================================================

5.1 CHEMICAL WASTE
  - Do not pour chemicals down the drain unless explicitly approved by the Safety Officer.
  - Label all waste containers with: contents, concentration, date, generator name.
  - Segregate by hazard class: flammables, halogenated solvents, acids, bases, heavy metals.
  - Contact EH&S for pickup when containers reach 75% capacity.

5.2 BIOLOGICAL AND SHARPS WASTE
  - Dispose of sharps (needles, broken glass, pipette tips) in puncture-resistant sharps containers ONLY.
  - Biological waste must be autoclaved before disposal or placed in biohazard bags for incineration.
  - Do NOT recap needles.
