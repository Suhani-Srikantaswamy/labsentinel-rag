SECTION 1: SAFETY PRECAUTIONS — READ BEFORE USE
=============================================================================

1.1 CRITICAL SAFETY RULES
  WARNING: Failure to follow these instructions may result in serious injury, equipment 
  damage, or rotor failure.

  a. ALWAYS balance samples before starting a run. Imbalanced loads cause rotor wobble, 
     vibration, and potential rotor failure. Balance to within ±0.1g across opposing positions.
  b. NEVER open the lid during operation. The centrifuge lid interlock must be functional.
     Do not bypass the safety interlock under any circumstances.
  c. NEVER exceed the maximum speed (RPM) specified for your rotor. Consult the rotor 
     specification table (Appendix A) before setting speed.
  d. ALWAYS check tubes for cracks or damage before loading. Use only centrifuge-grade tubes 
     rated for the intended speed and temperature.
  e. NEVER centrifuge flammable or volatile solvents in standard rotors — risk of explosion 
     due to motor sparks.
  f. Use aerosol-containment rotors when centrifuging biohazardous materials.

1.2 REQUIRED PPE
  - Safety goggles (chemical splash protection if centrifuging hazardous liquids)
  - Lab coat
  - Gloves appropriate to sample type

=============================================================================
SECTION 2: OPERATING PROCEDURE
=============================================================================

Step 1: Pre-Run Checks
  1.1 Inspect the rotor for cracks, corrosion, or damage — do NOT use damaged rotors.
  1.2 Check that the rotor is properly seated and secured on the drive shaft.
  1.3 Ensure the centrifuge chamber is clean and dry.

Step 2: Sample Loading
  2.1 Fill tubes to no more than 80% capacity to prevent leakage under centrifugal force.
  2.2 Place balanced pairs of tubes in OPPOSING positions.
  2.3 For odd numbers of samples, use a water-filled balance tube of equal weight.
  2.4 Close and latch all rotor covers or buckets securely.

Step 3: Setting Parameters
  3.1 Close the centrifuge lid.
  3.2 Set desired speed, time, and temperature.
  3.3 Confirm the selected rotor matches the rotor loaded (programmed rotor setting).
  3.4 Press START — verify that the RPM increases smoothly without unusual vibration.

Step 4: During the Run
  4.1 Remain near the centrifuge for the first minute to detect abnormal vibration.
  4.2 If unusual vibration, noise, or shaking occurs — press STOP immediately.
  4.3 Do NOT open the lid until the rotor has come to a complete stop (0 RPM).

Step 5: Post-Run
  5.1 Wait for complete stop — confirmed by RPM display reading 0.
  5.2 Open the lid carefully and remove samples.
  5.3 Inspect for leaks. If leakage occurred: contain, decontaminate per sample type SOP.
  5.4 Wipe the rotor and chamber with appropriate disinfectant.

SECTION 4: TROUBLESHOOTING
=============================================================================

  Symptom                    | Cause                    | Action
  ---------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------
  Excessive vibration        | Imbalanced load          | Stop, rebalance samples
  Lid won't open             | Rotor still spinning     | Wait — NEVER force the lid
  Error code E01             | Imbalance detected       | Stop, rebalance, restart
  Error code E05             | Over-temperature         | Check refrigeration; contact service
  Rotor wobble sound         | Rotor not seated         | Stop, reattach rotor, restart
  Sample leakage             | Overfilled / cracked tube| Decontaminate, replace tube

SECTION 5: MAINTENANCE AND INSPECTION SCHEDULE
=============================================================================

  Daily:   Wipe chamber; inspect rotor visually
  Weekly:  Lubricate rotor O-rings; clean with 70% ethanol
  Monthly: Full rotor inspection; log in maintenance record
  Annual:  Professional service and rotor recertification
