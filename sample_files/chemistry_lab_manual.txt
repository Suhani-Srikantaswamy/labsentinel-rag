SECTION 1 — GENERAL LABORATORY RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1  No food, drink, or chewing gum is permitted in the laboratory at any time.
1.2  Long hair must be tied back before entering the lab.
1.3  Closed-toe shoes must be worn — sandals, flip-flops, or bare feet are prohibited.
1.4  Never work alone. A second person must be present when using hazardous chemicals.
1.5  Mobile phone use during active experiments is prohibited.
1.6  Running inside the laboratory is strictly forbidden.
1.7  All spills, accidents, near-misses, and injuries must be reported to the supervisor
     immediately, regardless of how minor they appear.
1.8  Unauthorised persons must not enter the laboratory without prior approval.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2 — PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1  MANDATORY PPE at all times in the laboratory:
     - Laboratory coat (cotton or fire-resistant, fully fastened)
     - Safety glasses or chemical splash goggles
     - Nitrile gloves (latex-free) when handling any chemical

2.2  CHEMICAL SPLASH GOGGLES (not just safety glasses) are required when:
     - Pouring, transferring, or measuring any liquid chemical
     - Opening concentrated acid or base containers
     - Performing any reaction that may generate splashes or vapours

2.3  DOUBLE GLOVING is required when handling:
     - Concentrated acids (HCl >10%, H₂SO₄, HNO₃, HF)
     - Concentrated bases (NaOH >2M, KOH)
     - Acutely toxic substances (cyanides, mercury compounds)

2.4  Gloves must be removed before:
     - Touching door handles, keyboards, or shared surfaces
     - Leaving the laboratory area

2.5  A face shield (in addition to goggles) is required when:
     - Working with HF (hydrofluoric acid) at any concentration
     - Handling cryogenic liquids (liquid nitrogen, dry ice)
     - Performing high-pressure reactions

SECTION 3 — CHEMICAL HANDLING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1  Read the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for every chemical before use.
     SDS binders are located at the main lab entrance and the supervisor's office.

3.2  ACID HANDLING:
     - Always add acid TO water, never water to acid (exothermic reaction risk).
     - Concentrated acids must only be used inside a functioning fume hood.
     - Acid dilution must be performed in a heat-resistant borosilicate beaker.
     - Neutralising agent (sodium bicarbonate) must be within reach before starting.

3.3  FLAMMABLE SOLVENTS:
     - Keep only the minimum working quantity on the bench (max 500 mL).
     - Flammable solvents must be stored in approved flammable-liquid cabinets.
     - No open flames within 3 metres of any flammable solvent.
     - Use only spark-proof equipment (stirrers, hotplates) near flammables.

3.4  UNLABELLED CONTAINERS:
     - Never use chemicals from an unlabelled container.
     - All containers, including temporary ones, must be labelled with:
       chemical name, concentration, date prepared, preparer's name.

3.5  CHEMICAL INCOMPATIBILITIES:
     - Oxidisers (KMnO₄, H₂O₂, HNO₃) must never be stored with flammables.
     - Bleach (NaOCl) must never be mixed with ammonia, amines, or acids.
     - Strong acids and strong bases must be stored in separate cabinets.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4 — FUME HOOD OPERATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1  The following activities MUST be performed inside a functioning fume hood:
     - All work with volatile organic solvents (acetone, DCM, methanol, ether, THF)
     - Preparation or use of concentrated acids or bases
     - Any procedure that generates aerosols, fumes, dust, or toxic vapours
     - Heating reactions involving hazardous chemicals

4.2  Before beginning work in the fume hood:
     - Verify the airflow indicator is active (green light ON, or bead chain moving).
     - Lower the sash to the working height line (marked with a yellow sticker ≈ 45 cm).
     - Never work with the sash fully raised.

4.3  The sash must remain at or below the working height mark during ALL fume hood work.
     A fully raised sash provides NO protection.

4.4  If the fume hood alarm sounds:
     1. Stop work immediately.
     2. Close all containers.
     3. Lower the sash fully.
     4. Evacuate the immediate area.
     5. Call the safety officer: Ext. 4-SAFE.

4.5  Do not use a fume hood for storage — cluttered hoods restrict airflow.

SECTION 5 — EQUIPMENT SAFETY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1  CENTRIFUGE:
     - Always balance opposing sample positions before starting (weight within ±0.1 g).
     - For odd sample numbers, use a water-filled balance tube of equal weight.
     - Never exceed the rated maximum RPM for the rotor in use.
     - Never open the lid until the rotor has come to a complete stop (0 RPM).
     - Inspect rotor and tubes for cracks before every use.

5.2  HOTPLATE / STIRRER:
     - Never leave heating reactions unattended.
     - Use a clamp and ring stand — never hold hot glassware by hand.
     - Always check that hotplates are off before leaving the bench.

5.3  GLASSWARE:
     - Inspect glassware for cracks or chips before every use.
     - Dispose of broken glassware immediately in the designated sharps/glass bin.
     - Never use cracked or chipped glassware — it fails under thermal or pressure stress.

5.4  AUTOCLAVE / PRESSURE VESSELS:
     - Only trained, certified personnel may operate autoclave equipment.
     - Never autoclave sealed containers — pressure build-up causes explosion.

SECTION 6 — FIRE AND EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6.1  In the event of a fire:
     - For small, contained fires: use the nearest CO₂ extinguisher (PASS technique).
     - For any large fire or fire involving chemicals: activate the alarm, evacuate
       immediately, and call 999 / 911. Do not attempt to fight a large fire.
     - Never use water on chemical or electrical fires.

6.2  CHEMICAL SPILL — small (< 1 L, non-toxic):
     1. Alert others nearby.
     2. Don PPE (goggles, gloves, lab coat).
     3. Use the spill kit to absorb and neutralise.
     4. Dispose of waste in the appropriate labelled container.
     5. Complete an incident report form within 24 hours.

6.3  CHEMICAL SPILL — large or toxic/flammable:
     1. Evacuate the laboratory immediately.
     2. Close the door (do not lock).
     3. Activate the fire alarm if fire risk is present.
     4. Call Emergency Services and the Safety Officer.
     5. Do not re-enter until cleared by the Hazmat team.

6.4  EYE EXPOSURE to chemicals:
     - Go to the nearest eyewash station immediately.
     - Flush continuously for a minimum of 15 minutes.
     - Keep eyelids open during flushing.
     - Seek medical attention after flushing.

6.5  SKIN EXPOSURE:
     - Remove contaminated clothing immediately.
     - Wash affected area with soap and water for minimum 15 minutes.
     - Seek medical attention for any acid, base, or toxic substance exposure.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7 — WASTE DISPOSAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7.1  Never pour chemicals down the sink unless explicitly approved by the supervisor.

7.2  Segregate waste by hazard class:
     - Halogenated solvents (DCM, chloroform) — red labelled containers
     - Non-halogenated solvents (ethanol, acetone) — yellow labelled containers
     - Aqueous acid/base — neutralise to pH 6–8, then pour to drain with large water flush
     - Heavy metals — dedicated heavy-metal waste container

7.3  Sharps (broken glass, needles, blades) — puncture-resistant sharps container only.
     Do not place sharps in regular waste bins.

7.4  Notify the safety officer when any waste container reaches 75% full.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 8 — HOUSEKEEPING AND END OF SESSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8.1  Clean and dry the bench before leaving the laboratory.
8.2  Return all chemicals to their designated storage locations.
8.3  Ensure all hotplates, stirrers, and other electrical equipment are switched off.
8.4  Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water before leaving.
8.5  The last person to leave must verify: all gas taps closed, all electrical equipment
     off, fume hood sashes lowered, windows closed, laboratory door locked.
